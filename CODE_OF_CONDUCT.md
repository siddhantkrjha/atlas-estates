Atlas Estates Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of Atlas Estates, are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels and backgrounds. We pledge to communicate professionally, treat others with respect, and help maintain a constructive open-source community.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward others.

Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.

Giving and accepting constructive feedback professionally.

Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Focusing on what benefits the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats.

Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or derogatory comments.

Unwelcome sexual language, attention, or advances.

Publishing another person's private or identifying information without permission.

Deliberately disrupting project discussions or collaboration.

Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

These examples are not exhaustive. Behavior that undermines a safe and respectful project environment may also be addressed.

Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

Correction: A private request to stop inappropriate behavior.

Warning: A formal warning explaining the violation and consequences of continued behavior.

Temporary restriction: A temporary restriction from participating in project discussions or contributing.

Permanent restriction: Removal of permission to participate in project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer will aim to handle reports fairly and communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Atlas Estates project spaces, including:

GitHub issues and discussions.

Pull requests and code reviews.

Repository contributions and project documentation.

Other online spaces where individuals are officially representing Atlas Estates.

It applies to maintainers, contributors, collaborators, and community participants.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Private reporting contact: siddhantkumar345@hotmail.com

Please do not publish sensitive reports, personal information, or harassment complaints in public GitHub issues or discussions.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible. The maintainer will respect the privacy of those reporting incidents and will share information only when reasonably necessary to investigate and address the matter.
